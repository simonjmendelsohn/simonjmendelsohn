# Simon Mendelsohn

CTO and co-founder of [Apers](https://apers.app), where I build AI systems for institutional real estate investing: turning deal documents into working Excel models, memos, and research.

- **AI evaluation research** (independent):
  - ["About a quarter of them contained bugs": Gemini 4's ML R&D benchmark](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/7jmfDBu2GLCfdwSte/about-a-quarter-of-them-contained-bugs-gemini-4-s-ml-r-and-d). The benchmark Google DeepMind uses to rule out its AI self-improvement thresholds has, by the report's own audit, about a quarter of its scored tasks unpassable.
  - [Good Evals Are Hard: An ImpossibleBench Audit](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/qnrjAhiqire7JeoN2/good-evals-are-hard-an-impossiblebench-audit) ([code and data](https://github.com/simonjmendelsohn/impossiblebench-honest-failure)). In a benchmark for AI coding agents gaming their tests, 13 of 103 supposedly solvable control tasks have defective tests, and the cheat metric counts agents trying to stop as cheating. Reported upstream in [safety-research/impossiblebench#5](https://github.com/safety-research/impossiblebench/issues/5).
- **Before Apers:** Senior Software Engineer at Watershed Bio; created [sfkit](https://sfkit.org) at the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard, a platform for secure, federated genomic analysis ([NAR 2023](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkad464)); machine learning infrastructure at Amazon. Earlier, NLP research at Yale on neural networks with differentiable stacks.
- **Education:** B.S. in Statistics & Data Science and Computer Science, Yale.
